Smart Nation journey

Vision

  • Singapore’s Smart Nation initiative, led by the Ministry of Digital Development and Information (MDDI), aims to build a thriving digital future for everyone.

  • Launched in 2014, Smart Nation 1.0 sought to improve lives through seamless and meaningful use of technology.

  • Since its launch, Singapore has integrated digital technologies deeply into daily life, creating new opportunities while also needing to manage risks to well-being and social cohesion.

  • In 2024, Singapore introduced Smart Nation 2.0, a refreshed vision focused on using technology more effectively to transform the nation’s future.

  • The new phase shifts from primarily building digital capabilities to applying them strategically to shape Singapore’s long-term development.

Initiatives

  • Build for Good is a citizen engagement initiative by Open Government Products that encourages citizens to help improve Singapore.

  • People can participate through a hackathon, an accelerator for winning teams, or by joining the community.

  • The initiative aims to create a supportive space where individuals feel empowered to contribute positively, experiment, and build solutions that make the world better.

  • Hack for Public Good (HFPG) replaces top-down product development with a community-driven approach.

  • Instead of leadership setting the direction, tech teams engage directly with the community to identify public good problems in Singapore.

  • These teams then design and build solutions based on what they discover.

Streamlining Payroll Submissions in Singapore: APEX’s Solution for Efficient Government Reporting

The Problem

  • Singapore-registered companies must regularly submit employee salary, workforce, and CPF contribution data to various government agencies (IRAS, MOM, CPFB).

  • All required data already exists within companies’ payroll or HR systems.

  • The challenge is to integrate government submissions with these systems to streamline processes, reduce manual work, and save employers time and resources.

The solution

  • APEX unifies APIs from multiple government agencies on one secure platform.

  • It enables developers, including HR and payroll software providers, to integrate easily with these services.

  • APEX removes the need for manual filing reducing administrative burden and supports better compliance with payroll-related regulations.
